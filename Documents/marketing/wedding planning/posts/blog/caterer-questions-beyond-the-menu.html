Beyond the menu: what to ask a wedding caterer before you pay a deposit

Topics in this guide

couplescaterersvenuesStage: Celebrant & suppliersAustraliacost anxietyconfidence

Every caterer will tell you the food is delicious. What decides whether your reception runs smoothly is everything else: how many staff are serving, when dessert comes out and what happens when your guest count changes. Ask these questions, and ask for the answers in the quote.

Price and what it covers

  • What is the per-head price, and does it include GST, staffing, service charges, crockery, cutlery, linen and glassware? Our cost-per-head guide explains why headline prices mislead.
  • Is there a minimum spend or minimum number of guests?
  • Do prices change for Sundays, public holidays or events after a certain time?
  • What is the cancellation, postponement and refund policy?

Menu and tasting

  • Can we taste the dishes we are actually likely to serve, not just a sample platter? Is there a charge, and is it deducted if we book?
  • Can the menu change with the season or product availability, and who approves a swap?
  • Do you do shared tables, alternate drop, cocktail style or a station? How does service differ?

Dietary needs and allergies

  • How do you handle coeliac, vegan, halal, kosher and nut allergies? Is the kitchen set up to avoid cross-contact?
  • How do you identify dietary meals at the table so the right person gets the right plate?
  • Is there a child menu, and what age does it apply to?
  • Can you cater for vendors and the wedding party, and what is the vendor meal price?

Staffing and timing

  • How many staff are allocated per guest, and are they trained in the Responsible Service of Alcohol if they pour drinks?
  • How long does it take to serve a table, and what happens between courses? Slow service creates speech and photo delays.
  • Who is the on-the-day contact, and do they stay until the end of the night?
  • What time do you arrive and what do you need from the venue, such as kitchen access, power and refrigeration?

Numbers, changes and leftovers

  • When do final numbers and dietary details have to be confirmed, and what happens to numbers added or dropped after that?
  • What happens to leftover food? Some caterers will pack boxes for guests or the couple, others are restricted by food-safety rules.
  • If we want a late-night snack or a cake cut and served, what is the charge?

Paperwork

  • Can we see your food business registration or licence, and a certificate of currency for public liability insurance?
  • Does the venue allow outside caterers, and do they require approval or a fee? See venue catering rules.

If a caterer is vague on staffing or numbers, treat it as a signal, not an oversight. Before paying a deposit, read the contract against our vendor contract checklist.
