Capacity, catering and wet-weather plans at NSW wedding venues

Topics in this guide

couplesvenuescaterersStage: Venue & dateNSWclarityconfidence

Guest numbers drive almost every other venue decision: which rooms are possible, which catering model you get and what you do if it rains. We looked at what NSW venues publish on seated capacity, kitchens and wet-weather plans.

Key figures

  • 200 is the median seated capacity across 80 NSW venues with a stated figure. Half seat between about 120 and 275.
  • 94% of venues cater in-house: 127 of the 135 venues where we recorded the catering model. Eight work with outside caterers.
  • 14 of 18 venues with a recorded rain plan have a fully equivalent indoor alternative. Three have a partial one and one has none.
  • 111 of 176 venues (63%) have both indoor and outdoor space, and 58 offer on-site accommodation.

The smallest venue in this set seats 48 and the largest 700. Capacity differs by setting too: city hotels have a median of 240 seated, waterfront venues 170, and heritage garden estates 146. If your list is under 60, you will have fewer venues to choose from than couples with 100 to 250 guests, so look at restaurants and boutique spaces early.

Why this matters for cost: Dockside Group, a Sydney venue operator, puts it plainly.

"Guest numbers are the single biggest lever in a wedding budget."

Dockside Group (venue operator, Sydney), "How Much Does A Wedding Cost In Sydney", 24 June 2026.

The takeaway: in NSW you will usually eat what the venue's kitchen cooks. Venues that let you bring a caterer or your own drinks are a minority. If a specific caterer or a particular menu matters to you, make it your first filter, not your last.

"Equivalent" means the backup holds the same ceremony or reception without a major compromise. We recorded a rain plan for only 18 of 176 venues, so ask every venue you shortlist. One Sydney venue that sells itself as a backup, Avalon Beach Surf Life Saving Club, writes:

"As a highly sought-after wet-weather backup venue with a stunning oceanfront, all-weather setting, reserving the space offers peace of mind in the lead-up to your special day…"

Avalon Beach SLSC (venue), "Wet Weather Back Up".

The same page explains how the decision works on the day:

"On the day, you're welcome to make the final decision whether to proceed with your outdoor plans or move into the comfort of our venue."

Avalon Beach SLSC, same page.

A checklist for the venue visit

  • What is the comfortable seated capacity with a dance floor, not the maximum?
  • Is catering in-house only, and can dietary needs be met?
  • If it rains, where does the ceremony go, and who decides and when?
  • Is on-site accommodation available, and is it included or extra?

Pair this with the curfew and package hours data when you shortlist, and keep your answers in one place so you can compare venues side by side.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical seated capacity of a wedding venue in NSW?

The median seated capacity is 200 across 80 NSW venues with a stated figure. Half seat between about 120 and 275, and the range runs from 48 to 700 (OmnyID NSW venue dataset, October 2026).

Do NSW wedding venues have their own caterers?

Mostly yes. Of 135 venues where we recorded the catering model, 127 (94%) cater in-house and 8 use outside caterers. Only about half of the venues that state a policy allow outside caterers.

Do wedding venues in NSW have a wet-weather plan?

Of 18 venues with a recorded rain plan, 14 have an equivalent indoor alternative, 3 have a partial one and 1 has none. We recorded a rain plan for only 18 of 176 venues, so ask before booking.

How many NSW venues have both indoor and outdoor space?

In our dataset, 111 of 176 venues (63%) have both indoor and outdoor space, and 58 offer on-site accommodation.

How we built this data

OmnyID Weddings keeps a directory of NSW wedding venues, mostly around Sydney with some Hunter Valley, Blue Mountains and coastal venues. For each one we record what the venue publishes on its own website and public listings: starting package prices, capacity, finish times, catering model, and whether a rain plan exists. Descriptions are written in our own words. This snapshot is from October 2026 and covers 176 venues. Not every venue publishes every detail, so each figure below states how many venues it is based on.

We left out any value we had to estimate, so every number here is something a venue has stated. Venue prices change, and "from" prices usually exclude extras, so treat these as a benchmark for shortlisting, not a quote. Confirm everything with the venue before you pay a deposit.
