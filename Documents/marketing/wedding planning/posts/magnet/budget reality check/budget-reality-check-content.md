# Wedding Budget Reality Check — published content source record

The `budget reality check` source folder was empty, but the content has already
been implemented and published in `wedding/wedding-cost-calculator.html` with
its deterministic data and calculation code under `wedding/assets/js/`.

## Public promise

Build a practical Australian wedding allocation from a target total, location,
guest count and priorities. Show the method, adjustment bands, trade-offs and
costs that are often missed. Do not imply that a benchmark is a quote.

## Required content retained by the live page

- How the budget method works.
- Target total allocated to 100%.
- Category adjustment bands rather than contradictory percentages.
- Published model version and source/method context.
- Plan-versus-quote distinction.
- Missing-cost prompts and contingency.
- A path to try a real quote without claiming that unknown costs are zero.
