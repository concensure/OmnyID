## Commercial Thesis

The wedge is not "another wedding checklist."

The wedge is:

**OmnyID turns wedding planning into connected AFP memory: every recommendation, shortlist, budget row, task, date, email draft, approval, decision, and risk loop becomes inspectable, searchable, exportable, and reusable.**

The pitch should emphasize that wedding planning is a high-stress, high-spend, multi-stakeholder workflow where couples lose control because information is scattered across chats, spreadsheets, vendor websites, calendars, emails, and family discussions.

## TAM Working Frame

TAM should be presented carefully because there are several possible market definitions.

### Bottom-Up AI Planner TAM

Working assumptions:

- Major-market weddings per year: approximately 2.5M.
- Average active planning period: 12 months.
- Addressable couple-months: approximately 30M.
- Target subscription: USD 30/month.

Working calculation:

- 30M couple-months x USD 30/month = USD 900M annualized planner subscription opportunity.

Use case:

- This is the cleanest SaaS-style TAM for a subscription AI planner.

Caveat:

- It excludes upsell, marketplace, vendor, registry, and professional-planner workflows.

### Services-Adjacent TAM

Working assumptions:

- Global wedding services market placeholder: USD 265.46B.
- Planning influence/capture range: 5-10%.

Working calculation:

- USD 265.46B x 5% = USD 13.27B.

Use case:

- This is a broader "planning-influenced spend" frame.

Caveat:

- Do not imply OmnyID captures services revenue unless there is a clear business model for marketplace, referral, or transaction revenue.

Recommended internal TAM range:

- Conservative SaaS frame: about USD 900M.
- Broader services-adjacent frame: about USD 10-13B, only with strong caveats.

## SAM Working Frame

Initial serviceable market should stay narrower than global TAM.

Working geography:

- Australia first.
- Expand to UK, New Zealand, Canada, and other English-speaking markets after the template proves retention.

Working assumptions:

- Australian weddings per year: about 118,804.
- Couples actively planning at any time: about 120,000-140,000.
- Premium AI planner ARPU: AUD 39/month or AUD 468/year.

Working Australia SAM:

- 130,000 active planning couples x AUD 468/year = AUD 60.8M/year.

Working English-speaking SAM:

- 500,000 weddings/year x USD 468/year = USD 234M/year.

Recommended internal SAM range:

- Australia wedge: about AUD 60M/year.
- Australia + UK + Canada + NZ working frame: about USD 200-250M/year.

Caveat:

- This assumes direct-to-couple subscription. If OmnyID later sells to planners, venues, or celebrants, the SAM should be recalculated.

## SOM Working Frame

SOM should be grounded in acquisition capacity, not only market size.

### Year 1: Australia

Working assumptions:

- 1-2% of Australian active planning couples.
- Midpoint: 1.5%.
- 130,000 active couples x 1.5% = 1,950 paying couples.
- 1,950 x AUD 468/year = AUD 912,600 ARR.

Recommended pitch shorthand:

- Year 1 SOM: about AUD 1M ARR.

### Year 2: Australia + UK Expansion

Working assumptions:

- 500,000 annual weddings in broader English-speaking focus.
- 3-5% penetration target.
- Midpoint: 3.5%.
- 17,500 paying couples x USD 468/year = about USD 8.2M ARR.

Recommended pitch shorthand:

- Year 2 SOM: about USD 8M ARR.

### Year 3: Multi-Market Scale

Working assumption:

- 8-10% penetration in core active markets after product-market fit and distribution mature.

Recommended pitch shorthand:

- Year 3 SOM: USD 20-25M ARR.

Caveat:

- These are ambition-case planning numbers, not forecasts. They require distribution, retention, payment conversion, support, and trust signals that are not yet proven.

## Pricing Working Frame

Potential pricing:

- AUD/USD 39-49/month for a premium AI planner.
- Annual discount or event-period package could reduce churn friction.
- A one-time wedding packet price could work for couples who dislike subscriptions.

Suggested packaging:

| Package | Working price | Fit |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter planning packet | AUD 49-99 one-time | First plan, budget, venue shortlist, task/date setup |
| Full wedding workspace | AUD 39-49/month | Ongoing planning, approvals, exports, memory, follow-ups |
| Premium planning sprint | AUD 149-299 one-time | High-touch week with vendor drafts and decision support |

Do not overbuild pricing until the core workflow shows repeat usage.

## Product Proof Points To Pitch

The strongest pitch points should map to implemented or planned product behaviors:

- AFP-native memory: planning files, decisions, tasks, calendar events, approvals, and exports are durable messages, not throwaway chat.
- Artifact evidence status: each opened planning file shows whether it is source-linked, source-labelled, or still needs verification; weakly sourced consequential artifacts also become graph risks before users rely on pricing, availability, legal, map, registry, or vendor claims.
- Budget evidence check: budget previews show unknown/TBC costs, due amounts, unpaid rows, and warning rows without inventing totals.
- Second-order loops: weather backup, vendor follow-up, legal/deposit/contract, guest logistics, and budget-drift risks become tasks or graph nodes.
- Decision scope: users can tick a venue/vendor/celebrant option, collapse alternatives, and generate follow-up reports from the selected path.
- Approval gates: emails, vendor contact, sharing, and sensitive exports are blocked until explicit approval and provider readiness.
- Exportable outputs: Markdown, printable HTML/Save-as-PDF, Word-openable `.doc`, CSV where appropriate, with native ZIP/DOCX/JPG/PNG kept behind real workers.
- Existing OmnyID surfaces: Files, Calendar, Tasks, Knowledge Graph, Search, Decision Queue, and Response Queue are reused rather than creating a siloed wedding app.

## Slide Framing

Recommended slide headline:

**Wedding planning is expensive, fragmented, and consequence-heavy. OmnyID turns it into connected memory with approvals, tasks, dates, exports, and risk loops.**

Recommended support copy:

- Couples do not only need ideas; they need decisions, dates, files, emails, budgets, and follow-ups to stay connected.
- OmnyID can start with a planning response and grow into a private wedding operations room.
- The same architecture can later support other high-stakes life-event templates.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim live vendor availability.
- Do not claim legal compliance, NOIM lodgement, payment, deposit, or booking confirmation.
- Do not claim overspend reduction until measured.
- Do not imply partnerships with venues, celebrants, registries, or government sources unless they exist.
- Do not quote market figures externally without source links and dates.
