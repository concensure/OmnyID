## **1. Decision Fatigue vs. Vendor Search: Which Is the Bigger Pain Point?**

### **Decision Fatigue in Wedding Planning**
- **What It Is**: The **mental exhaustion** from making countless decisions (e.g., venue, catering, seating, invitations, music, attire, etc.). Studies show that the average couple makes **over 1,000 decisions** during wedding planning, leading to **analysis paralysis, stress, and even conflict**.
- **Why It’s a Bigger Concern**:
  - **Emotional Toll**: Decision fatigue can **strain relationships** (e.g., arguments over guest lists or budgets).
  - **Time-Consuming**: Couples spend **hours debating options** (e.g., "Should we do a buffet or plated dinner?").
  - **Overwhelm**: Many couples feel **unprepared** for the sheer volume of choices, leading to **procrastination or regret**.
  - **Psychological Impact**: The **pressure to "get it perfect"** amplifies stress, especially for high-stakes decisions like venues or photographers.

- **Data**:
  - **60% of couples** report feeling **overwhelmed by the number of decisions** (The Knot, 2025).
  - **45% of couples** say decision fatigue was their **biggest wedding planning challenge** (WeddingWire, 2024).
  - **30% of couples** **delay planning** due to decision paralysis (Zola, 2024).

---

### **Finding Vendors**
- **What It Is**: The **logistical challenge** of researching, vetting, and booking vendors (e.g., photographers, caterers, florists).
- **Why It’s Still Important**:
  - **Trust Issues**: Couples worry about **quality, reliability, and reviews** (e.g., "Will this photographer show up on time?").
  - **Time-Consuming**: Researching vendors can take **dozens of hours** (e.g., reading reviews, comparing prices, scheduling consultations).
  - **Budget Constraints**: Finding vendors that **fit their budget and style** is a major stressor.
  - **Availability**: Popular vendors (e.g., photographers, venues) book up **12–18 months in advance**, adding pressure.

- **Data**:
  - **50% of couples** say **finding the right vendors** was a top challenge (The Knot, 2025).
  - **35% of couples** **regret their vendor choices** (e.g., photographer, DJ) due to rushed decisions or lack of research (WeddingWire, 2024).
  - **25% of couples** **hire a wedding planner** primarily to **handle vendor coordination** (Zola, 2024).
