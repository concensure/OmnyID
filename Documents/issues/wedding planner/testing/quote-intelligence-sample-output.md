# Five-venue quote intelligence sample

Generated by `cargo run --example wedding_quote_sample_comparison -- <sample-directory>` from the five reviewed TXT sidecars supplied for Priya Raman and Daniel Whitfield. The PDF files remain the source documents; the TXT files are their reviewable extraction layer.

## Scenario held constant

- Couple: Priya Raman and Daniel Whitfield
- Event: Saturday 20 November 2027
- Guests: 90
- Currency: AUD
- Availability: not confirmed by any quotation

## Sample user output

### Five quotes compared — no booking recommendation yet

Every option still needs availability confirmed. Estimated, incomplete, and adjusted totals are labelled rather than treated as equivalent fixed prices.

| Venue | Comparable amount | Confidence in total | What still needs attention |
|---|---:|---|---|
| The Boathouse at Balmoral Cove | $30,350.00 | Complete stated total | Confirm availability. The stated $7,500 deposit does not equal 25% of the total; 25% would be $7,587.50. |

| Bayview Pavilion | From $29,500.00 | Incomplete | Confirm the exact date, availability, final price for 90 guests, deposit, due dates, and package detail. The “from” figure is not treated as the cheapest comparable quote. |

All five options remain **conditional**, not “recommended” or “bookable”, because availability for the requested date is unknown.

## Fairness assessment

This output is fair for an initial comparison because it:

- holds guest count, date and currency constant;
- does not treat a quote document as proof of availability;
- does not rank Bayview's incomplete “from” price as a fixed $29,500 offer;
- distinguishes Tallow Creek's consumption estimate from complete totals;
- adds Coldstream's source-stated mandatory security cost; and
- avoids a winner when a hard booking requirement is unknown for every candidate.

The comparison is not a suitability ranking. The supplied brief contains no target location, travel-time limit, overall venue budget, accessibility needs or atmosphere preferences. Adding any of those later could legitimately change the order. The extraction also needs human confirmation of the Balmoral deposit inconsistency and Coldstream's mandatory-charge interpretation before either is used for payment or contract decisions.

## Reproducibility note

The runner asserts that each supplied TXT sidecar contains the 90-guest/date evidence, then passes reviewed comparison candidates into the production `compare_quotations` function. It deliberately fails if the engine recommends a vendor while all availability requirements remain unknown. The source PDFs and TXT files are not copied into the application worktree.
