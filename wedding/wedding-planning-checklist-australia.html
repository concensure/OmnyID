How this is calculated

The catalogue contains 65 tasks across Plan & budget, Venue & date, Celebrant & suppliers, Details & invites, Final prep, and Wedding & after. Supplier research dates come from the planner's existing category rules, so the public checklist and signed-in planner do not disagree.

Supplier windows are estimates for when to start research—not claims that a supplier is available or that every couple must book at the same time. Short timelines retain overdue items under Catch up now rather than hiding them or showing unusable dates in the past.

The federal NOIM legal window is separate from the planning target. State-specific lodgement claims are deliberately omitted because the federal form is given to the authorised celebrant and local circumstances should be checked with that celebrant.
