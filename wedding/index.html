✦ How it works

From "where do we even start" to a plan you trust

Four calm steps. No spreadsheets to build from scratch, no forty-tab browser windows, no forgotten deposit dates.

1

Tell us the essentials

Rough date, region, guest count and budget comfort. Five questions, not fifty — we only ask more when it unlocks a real recommendation.

2

Get a rich, ready plan

A full picture arrives at once: budget snapshot, vendor shortlist, timeline, and the decisions that actually need your input.

3

Approve, don't guess

Every email, booking or share is a draft until you approve it. Nothing leaves your workspace without your say-so.

4

Everything, remembered

Vendors, quotes, dates and preferences stay linked and searchable — so replanning after a change takes minutes, not evenings.

✦ Why couples choose OmnyID

Everything a wedding touches, in one calm place

Not a rigid checklist app. A private planning partner that adapts to your wedding, your culture, and your pace while keeping every artifact searchable through OmnyID.

Private by design

Everything defaults to private. Sharing with your partner, planner, family or a vendor is always explicit — never automatic.

Deadlines that keep themselves

Deposits, NOIM timing, RSVP cut-offs and vendor calls land in your calendar automatically, with enough runway to actually act.

Budget that stays honest

Estimated vs. confirmed vs. paid, tracked per vendor — with hidden-cost warnings before they surprise you.

Vendor shortlists with real reasons

Every recommendation shows its source, fit, capacity, price, availability, risk flags and what still needs live verification.

Venues, vendors and guests, mapped

Nothing sent without you

Emails to vendors, celebrants and family are always drafts first. You read, adjust, and approve before anything leaves OmnyID.
