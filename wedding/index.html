Four calm steps. No spreadsheets to build from scratch, no forty-tab browser windows, no forgotten deposit dates.

  1. 1

    Tell us the essentials

✦ When a vendor replies

Vendor replies land in one place, with a reply ready for you to approve.

You don't have to dig through an inbox to find the quote, work out what it means for your budget, and then write back. OmnyID brings the reply to you as one decision, and drafts your answer. You stay in charge of what gets sent.

  1. 1

    The vendor replies

    Their quote arrives in the same thread as your enquiry. OmnyID pulls out the total, what's included and how long it's valid. If a quote is an attachment it can't read, it tells you rather than guessing.

  2. 2

    See what it means for your plan

    The quote is set against your budget, and anything over is flagged. You get a suggested next step with the reasons behind it, based on what you've told us matters to you.

  3. 3

    Review the drafted reply

    A reply is drafted for you to edit or send as it is. Before it can go, you approve the wording and confirm any budget impact.

  4. 4

    Send, with a moment to undo

    Nothing sends until you approve it. Once sent, there's a short window to undo, and the quote, reply and decision stay linked in your plan.

✦ Clear before you contact anyone

Questions couples should not have to dig for

OmnyID explains evidence, privacy and contact boundaries in the workspace when they matter. Here is the fuller version.

We show source-backed business details and distinguish an identified business from a suitable vendor. A verified website, listing or owner claim supports identity; it does not prove availability, price, capacity or fit for your wedding. Those remain clearly marked until confirmed.
The recommendation reasons shown to couples are based on stated requirements, preferences and available evidence—not a hidden payment signal. If OmnyID has a commercial relationship or sponsored placement, it must be labelled separately and must not be presented as suitability evidence.
A normalized quote is a readable projection of the original vendor document, not a new price. It shows the event basis, confirmed inclusions, required adjustments, exclusions and unanswered questions. Estimates, “from” prices, optional extras and missing tax are not treated as a complete comparable total.
Before sending, you see the recipient, exact message, attachments and a list of relevant wedding information being shared. Approximate budget and phone contact permission are separate choices. Protected OmnyID replies are the default intent; where that reviewed service is not enabled, sending stays blocked until you deliberately choose your connected email.
Phone permission starts off. OmnyID never adds a phone number to an enquiry automatically. The vendor receives only what the preview shows, and a reminder creates a task for you—it does not start an automatic follow-up campaign.
Drafts and recommendations must cite the structured wedding facts and vendor or quote sources they used. Missing facts stay unknown. You review every outbound draft, and the original quote remains available beside its normalized summary so you can check it.
You can ask OmnyID to remind you, send one reviewed follow-up, compare another shortlisted option or close the enquiry. OmnyID does not imply availability, keep contacting the vendor without approval or leave an unanswered enquiry looking like a booking.
Not exactly. Tasks are the full planning checklist, while the focus card can also include a decision, approval, vendor response or dated action that needs attention now. One task may contain no decision or several decisions. OmnyID keeps them linked in the same wedding ledger, but labels their counts separately so a task total is not mistaken for a decision total.
