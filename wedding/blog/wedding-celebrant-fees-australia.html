What does a wedding celebrant cost in Australia? The figures, and why they disagree

Topics in this guide

couplescelebrantsStage: Celebrant & suppliersAustraliacost anxietyconfidence

A celebrant is the one wedding supplier the law requires, and the one couples have the fewest price benchmarks for. Fees range from a few hundred dollars to well over a thousand, and the sources disagree. This guide pulls together the figures we could verify, shows why they don't match, and lists what to ask so you compare like with like.

Key figures

  • $1,031 is the average celebrant spend in Easy Weddings' 2026 report, among couples who hired one.
  • $794 was the national average in Easy Weddings' 2020 industry report. NSW was highest at $887 and Tasmania lowest at $515.
  • Other guides give wide ranges: Bridebook $500 to $1,500 and Oneflare $350 to $800 (as we saw them summarised).
  • Easy Weddings says to expect $1,000 or more for a customised, personalised ceremony.

Why the numbers don't line up

The figures come from different surveys, different years and different definitions of "celebrant cost". A 2020 average of $794 and a 2026 average of $1,031 are not the same measurement, so we wouldn't read the gap as one year's price rise. What the sources do agree on is the shape of the market: a low end for "legals only", a middle for a personalised ceremony, and a high end for experienced or in-demand celebrants.

What you're paying for

Easy Weddings describes two broad packages:

  • "Legals only". The legal paperwork and a short, standard ceremony. It describes this as cost-effective for simple, intimate weddings.
  • Personalised. A script written for you, with draft changes, meetings and advice. It says the average celebrant "puts 23 hours into every wedding", which is a good reminder that you're paying for time, not just the 20 minutes at the altar.

Things that push the price up: a custom script, readings or rituals, longer or religious ceremonies charged by the hour, and a celebrant's experience. Travel can add cost, because many celebrants have a set radius. Easy Weddings says additional charges are possible beyond it, though it gives no dollar amount.

The extra costs people forget

  • The official marriage certificate. That is a separate registry fee. One source we saw gives $67 for NSW, but fees change, so check your state's Births, Deaths and Marriages site.
  • Rehearsal. Ask whether it's included. We couldn't find a published benchmark.
  • Sound equipment and travel. Check whether the celebrant provides a microphone and speaker for outdoor ceremonies.

Questions that make quotes comparable

  1. Is the Notice of Intended Marriage paperwork included?
  2. How many meetings or calls are included?
  3. Do I get a written script, and how many drafts?
  4. Is a rehearsal included?
  5. Do you charge travel or time-based extras?
  6. Is a deposit refundable if I need to change the date? See your rights on deposits.
  7. Are you on the Attorney-General's register of authorised celebrants? Our guide to finding a celebrant shows how to check.

Our fuller list of celebrant questions goes into red flags.

Where it sits in your budget

At $1,031, the celebrant is roughly 2.7% of an average $38,252 wedding (a rough comparison, since the two figures average different groups of couples). It's one of the smaller lines in the budget, but it's the one with the most effect on how the ceremony feels. See the budget gap breakdown for the other lines.

Your turn: the celebrant shortlist

  • Ask three celebrants for the same package in writing.
  • Put them in a table: price, meetings, script, travel, rehearsal.
  • Pick the one you'd be happy to sit across a table from, not the cheapest.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding celebrant cost in Australia?

Easy Weddings' 2026 report puts the average celebrant spend at $1,031 among couples who hired one. Its 2020 industry report put the national average at $794. Other guides give ranges of $350 to $800 (Oneflare) and $500 to $1,500 (Bridebook).

Why are celebrant fees so different between sources?

The figures come from different surveys, years and definitions. Prices also vary with the package, travel and the celebrant's experience.

What does a celebrant fee include?

A basic package covers the legal paperwork and a standard ceremony. Personalised packages add a custom script, meetings and drafts. Travel, rehearsal and sound equipment may cost extra.

Is the marriage certificate included in the celebrant fee?

Usually not. The official marriage certificate is a separate registry fee, which varies by state and territory.
