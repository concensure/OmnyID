Flowers and music: what to ask your florist, DJ or band

Topics in this guide

couplesfloristsDJsbandsStage: Celebrant & supplierscreativityconfidence

Flowers and music have something in common: they are both easy to over-budget, and both depend on timing and access more than most couples realise. These two sets of questions cover the practical side.

Florist: what to ask

Design and season

  • Which flowers are in season and good value for our month? Australia's seasons are the reverse of the northern hemisphere, so what you see on overseas Pinterest boards may be out of season or imported.
  • Can you work from a budget and show us options at different price points?
  • If a flower is unavailable, what will you substitute and will you check with us first?

Logistics

  • What is included in the quote: bouquets, buttonholes, ceremony arrangements, tables, delivery, set-up and pack-down?
  • Are hired items such as vases, arches and stands included, and what is the damage or loss policy?
  • What time can you access the venue, and does the venue allow earlier delivery? Heat is a real problem for summer flowers waiting outside.
  • Can ceremony flowers be moved to the reception to save money, and who will move them?

Contract

  • When is the final order due and what happens if the guest count or table plan changes?
  • What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

DJ or band: what to ask

Music and requests

  • Can we provide a must-play and do-not-play list? How do you handle guest requests?
  • Can you play a ceremony processional and cocktail hour music, or is that a separate package?
  • If we choose a band, how long are the sets, who plays during their breaks and how do they handle volume near the dinner tables?

MC role and speeches

  • Will you act as MC, and how do you handle speeches and unexpected delays?
  • Can you work from our timeline and keep the program on schedule?

Sound, space and rules

  • What equipment do you bring: speakers, microphones, lighting? Do you need power points or extra space?
  • Do you know the venue's noise limit and curfew? Check against our curfew guide.
  • What happens if equipment fails? Do you carry backup gear and a backup performer?
  • What is the overtime rate if the party runs late?

Practical

  • Do you need a meal and a green room, or a private space for the band?
  • Do you hold public liability insurance and test-and-tag certificates for electrical gear?

One brief for both

Give your florist and your DJ the same run sheet and the same venue contact. If the florist needs the room at 10 am and the DJ needs the same space for a sound check, someone needs to know before the day. When the details are complete, put them in writing using our contract checklist.
