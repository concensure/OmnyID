Photographer and videographer: the questions that decide what you actually receive

Topics in this guide

couplesphotographersvideographersStage: Celebrant & suppliersAustraliauncertaintyconfidence

Photos and video are the main things you keep after the day. Our guide on choosing a photographer you will love in 20 years covers style and fit. This one is the practical checklist, written to cover both a photographer and a videographer.

Who will actually be there

  • Will you shoot our wedding personally? If not, who will, and can we see their work?
  • Do you use a second shooter, and are they included in the price?
  • What happens if you are ill or have an emergency? Is there a backup photographer and a written substitution clause?

Coverage and timeline

  • How many hours are included, and what does an extra hour cost?
  • Which parts of the day are covered: getting ready, ceremony, portraits, speeches, first dance?
  • How long do you need for couple portraits, and when is the best light for our season and location? Australian summer light can be harsh at midday.
  • Do you need a meal, and where can your team rest during the night?
  • If you shoot a staged exit or a drone shot, are there any restrictions? Check with the venue first.

What you will receive

  • How many edited photos, in what resolution, and how are they delivered: online gallery, download or USB?
  • For video: highlight film, full ceremony and speeches, raw footage? What is the length of each?
  • How long will editing take, and what delivery date will you put in writing? Some films take many months, particularly in peak season.
  • How long will the online gallery stay live, and can we download everything ourselves?

Copyright, printing and music

  • Who owns the copyright, and what are we allowed to do with the images and film: print, post online, share with guests?
  • Can we print anywhere, or only through your lab? Are there printing costs or restrictions?
  • For video, what music is used? Licensed tracks may limit where you can share the film.
  • May you use our images for your own marketing, and can we opt out?

Backups and risk

  • Do you record to two cards at once, and how are files backed up after the wedding?
  • How long do you keep the files after delivery?
  • Do you hold public liability and equipment insurance? Many venues need a certificate.

Payment and paperwork

  • What is the booking deposit and the payment schedule?
  • What is the cancellation and postponement policy? Many wedding vendors keep the deposit if you cancel, so check.
  • Can we have all of this in a written agreement before we pay? Use our contract checklist.

Do you need both?

Many couples book photography as the priority and either skip video or choose a shorter highlight film. If you do book both, ask whether they have worked together before, and brief them on how you want them to share space during the ceremony. A shared shot list and timeline helps everyone stay out of each other's way.
