What do Sydney wedding photographers charge? 20 published prices and what hours you get

Topics in this guide

couplesphotographersStage: Celebrant & suppliersSydneyNSWcost anxietyconfidence

Wedding photography is one of the few suppliers where the price on the website can be wildly different from what couples end up paying. Many photographers don't publish a price at all, and those that do quote a "from" figure for a set number of hours. We looked at what Sydney photographers publish, compared it with national surveys, and listed the questions that stop a quote from surprising you.

Key figures

  • Of 93 Sydney photographers we looked at, only 20 had a published starting price we could confirm. 37 published none.
  • Those 20 had a median starting price of about $2,250, with half between roughly $1,660 and $2,840. The range was $970 to $6,200.
  • Easy Weddings' 2026 report puts average photography spend at $3,567 and videography at $3,125, among couples who bought each.
  • Starting prices are for limited hours. The 8 that stated hours ranged from 2 to 6.

The sample is small, and photographers who publish a price may not represent the whole market. Easy Weddings' $3,567 average covers a much larger group of couples and includes bigger packages. The two numbers measure different things, which is why they differ.

Hours don't line up neatly with price, so a "from $1,649" for six hours and "from $2,890" for four are not comparable at a glance. Divide by the hours you actually need, then compare. One Sydney studio's pricing guide warns that budget tiers often offer only two to three hours, which may not cover both the ceremony and the reception. That guide is a vendor's own marketing, so weigh it accordingly.

Other benchmarks, with caveats

  • Bridebook (Australia) organises prices by band. Its lowest band, under $2,000, is roughly four hours with an online gallery. Its top band, $6,000 and over, covers up to two photographers for the whole day plus a premium album. We couldn't see a date on the data.
  • Another Australian guide puts a typical range at $2,000 to $6,000, with standard six to eight hour packages at about $2,500 to $4,000.

Sources: OmnyID Sydney photographer dataset (October 2026); Easy Weddings, 2026 cost report; Bridebook, "How Much Does Wedding Photography Cost in Australia?"; Top4, "Wedding Photography Cost in Sydney: 2026 Pricing Guide". Some of these are vendor or marketing pages.

Seven questions that make quotes comparable

  1. How many hours are included, and what does an extra hour cost?
  2. How many photographers, and who is shooting my wedding?
  3. How many edited photos will I receive, and by when?
  4. Do I get the full gallery or a selection? In what resolution?
  5. What are the usage rights? Can I print and share freely?
  6. Are albums, prints or an engagement shoot extra?
  7. What happens if you're ill on the day? Is there a backup photographer?

Where to spend and where to save

Our view, not a measured result: guarantee coverage of the ceremony and the key moments first. Extra hours at the end of the night usually matter less than a photographer you feel comfortable with.

Your turn: build your hours

  • Write your day's schedule and mark the moments you most want photographed.
  • Count the hours from the first to the last. That's the package size you need.
  • Ask three photographers to quote for exactly those hours.

How we built this data

OmnyID Weddings keeps a directory of Sydney wedding vendors. For each photographer we record what is published on their own website. This snapshot is from October 2026 and covers 93 photographers. We counted a price only when we could confirm it, and left out values we had to estimate. Starting prices exclude extras, and prices change, so treat these as a benchmark for shortlisting, not a quote.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Sydney?

Among 20 Sydney photographers with a confirmed published starting price, the median is about $2,250, with half between roughly $1,660 and $2,840. The range is $970 to $6,200 (OmnyID dataset, October 2026). Starting prices exclude extras.

What is the average spend on wedding photography in Australia?

Easy Weddings' 2026 report puts it at $3,567 among couples who bought photography, and $3,125 for videography.

Why do many wedding photographers not publish prices?

In our data, 37 of 93 Sydney photographers published no price. Many prefer to quote after learning the date, hours and style, so you may need to ask.

How many hours of photography do you need?

Count the hours from the first moment you want photographed to the last. Then ask each photographer to quote for exactly those hours.
