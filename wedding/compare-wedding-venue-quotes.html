      Common proposal phrases, decoded

      From A$X per head
      A starting figure. Ask which menu, drinks, staffing, day and minimum numbers it assumes.
      Minimum spend applies
      You may pay the minimum even when the items ordered total less. Ask what counts toward it and whether a shortfall is payable.
      Plus service
      The percentage and calculation base are incomplete. Ask for both in writing.
      Five-hour package
      Ask when the five hours start, whether service stops before the reception ends, and the extension rate.
      Subject to availability
      This is not a hold or booking. Ask the venue to confirm the specific date and any expiry.
      Exclusive use
      Ask which rooms, outdoor areas, setup period and pack-down period are actually exclusive.
