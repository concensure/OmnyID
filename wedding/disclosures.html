1. We are not affiliated with any vendor

OmnyID Weddings has no ownership, partnership, referral, or commission relationship with any venue, celebrant, photographer, caterer, florist, or other wedding supplier that may appear in your plan. Vendors listed in your workspace come from information you provide, public listings, or your own research — never from a paid placement arrangement. If a vendor ever pays us for anything in the future, that would require a clear, opt-in change to this page and a visible label wherever it applies — not a quiet update to fine print.

2. We will not push you towards purchases you didn't ask for

OmnyID's job is to help you make the decisions you've already decided to make — faster and with less stress — not to grow the size of your wedding budget. Concretely, that means:

  • We never surface a vendor, add-on, or upgrade because it would be profitable for us — we have no such incentive to begin with.
  • Budget suggestions are shown as ranges grounded in what you've told us about your priorities and your own stated ceiling, not as a nudge toward the higher end.
  • Follow-up items (like "get a quote for X") are only generated from decisions you've actually made or explicitly asked about — never invented to manufacture more spending.
  • You can decline, dismiss, or ignore any suggestion permanently, and OmnyID will not keep re-surfacing it.

3. How a recommendation actually gets made

When OmnyID suggests a next step, a vendor category, or a budget split, it's reasoning from the specific details you've shared for your wedding — your date, region, guest count, budget range, and stated priorities — combined with general planning knowledge (typical timelines, common dependencies between decisions, standard cost ranges). It is not reasoning from a paid catalogue, and it does not rank options by anything other than relevance to what you've told us matters to you.

4. AI-generated content: useful, not infallible

OmnyID uses AI to draft suggestions, summaries, and emails. Like any AI system, it can occasionally get a detail wrong, miss context, or phrase something in a way you wouldn't. That's exactly why every AI-drafted item is presented to you as a draft for review, not a final action — see §6. We'd rather you catch and correct something than trust it blindly, and the product is built around that assumption throughout, not as an afterthought.

5. No advertising, no sponsored placement

There is no advertising anywhere in the OmnyID Weddings product or workspace, and no vendor or category can pay to be shown, ranked higher, or featured more prominently. Our business model is the subscription you choose to pay (see pricing) — not advertising revenue, not data sales, and not vendor placement fees.

6. Nothing leaves your workspace without your say-so

OmnyID can prepare a draft email, a shortlist, or a summary on your behalf, but it never sends, books, or purchases anything automatically. Every sensitive action — contacting a vendor, sending an invitation, committing to a budget line — sits behind an explicit approval step you control. If something feels premature or off, you can edit or cancel it before it goes anywhere.

7. Pricing & subscription transparency

Our pricing is published, not negotiated per vendor deal or hidden behind a "contact us" wall. See the full breakdown on our pricing page. If you're on a free plan, it functions the same way as a paid one with respect to this page — free-tier users are never shown different, less-neutral recommendations to make up the difference.

8. Your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or planning data to any third party, including to vendors. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

9. Your consumer rights (Australia)

Where Australian Consumer Law applies, nothing on this page limits the consumer guarantees you're entitled to — including guarantees that services are provided with due care and skill, and that any digital product is fit for the purpose it's described for. If you believe OmnyID has fallen short of those guarantees, contact us using the details below and we'll work through it with you directly, before you need to escalate anywhere else.

10. Complaints & how we handle them

If a suggestion ever feels like it was steering you toward an unnecessary purchase, or you spot anything on this page that doesn't match what you experienced in the product, we want to know. Email us and we'll investigate directly — including, where relevant, reviewing the specific recommendation that concerned you — and respond within 5 business days.

11. Changes to this page

If we make a material change to how recommendations are generated, how we're funded, or any point on this page, we'll notify you by email and within the product before the change takes effect, not just by editing this page quietly.

12. Contact us

Questions about this page, or a specific recommendation you'd like us to look at? Reach us at hello@omnyid.com and we'll respond within 5 business days.
