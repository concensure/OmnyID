How OmnyID earns money, and what that never changes.

We are paid in three ways: by couples, by vendor commission, and by clearly labelled advertising and sponsorship. None of them decides which vendors we recommend to you. This page explains how that works in plain language. Last updated 10 October 2026.

  • Vendor commission is disclosed
  • Advertising is labelled "Advertisement"
  • Sponsored placement is labelled "Sponsored"
  • Recommendations are based on you

1. How OmnyID earns money

OmnyID Weddings has three sources of revenue:

  • Couples. A free plan, and a Couple's Plan for a one-off AUD $39. See pricing.
  • Vendor commission. A vendor may pay us a commission when you book them after finding or contacting them through OmnyID. See section 3.
  • Advertising and sponsorship. A vendor or brand may pay to advertise or to sponsor a placement. These are always labelled. See section 4.

OmnyID does not sell, rent or trade your personal or planning data to anyone. Data is not one of our revenue sources.

2. Why you are shown a vendor

The vendors OmnyID suggests to you are chosen because they look like the best fit for you. We work that out from what we know about your wedding and from what you have told us you want: your date, region, guest count, budget, stated priorities and preferences, and the evidence we hold about each vendor, such as location, capacity, price, availability and reviews.

  • Whether a vendor pays us commission does not make it rank higher, and a vendor that does not pay us is considered on the same terms.
  • A paid relationship is never a "fit" factor. It is not used to score, order or shortlist vendors.
  • When we recommend a vendor, we show the reasons, so you can judge the fit for yourself and disagree with it.
  • If you tell us a preference, such as a style, a suburb or a ceiling on cost, we apply it, even where that means a vendor with no commercial relationship with us comes first.

3. Vendor commission

If you book a vendor after finding or contacting them through OmnyID, that vendor may pay OmnyID a commission. The commission is paid by the vendor, not by you. It is not a charge to you and is not shown as a separate line on your quote.

To work out whether a commission is owed, OmnyID and the vendor may confirm that you reached the vendor through OmnyID. We do not share your budget, guest list, family notes or other plan details for that purpose. A vendor sees only what you choose to send them.

Not every vendor in OmnyID pays commission, and you can contact any vendor you find directly instead. Doing so costs you nothing extra.

4. Advertising and sponsored placement

Vendors and brands may pay us to advertise or to sponsor a placement. When they do:

  • Advertising is labelled "Advertisement". It is shown as an advertisement, in a visually separate space. It is never presented as a recommendation or as OmnyID's view.
  • Sponsored placement is labelled "Sponsored". The label sits on the item itself, in text, in the same place for every sponsored item, so it cannot be missed or mistaken for part of the ranking.
  • Payment does not change your recommendations. A sponsor can buy a labelled placement. It cannot buy a place in the list of vendors we recommend for you, and it cannot change the reasons we give for a recommendation.
  • Labels are not optional. If something is paid for and not labelled, that is a mistake. Tell us at hello@omnyid.com and we will fix it.

5. We will not push you towards purchases

OmnyID's job is to help you make the decisions you have already decided to make, faster and with less stress. It is not to grow your wedding budget. Concretely:

  • We do not surface a vendor, add-on or upgrade because it pays us. See section 2.
  • Budget suggestions are ranges grounded in what you have told us about your priorities and your own ceiling, not a nudge to the higher end.
  • Follow-up items such as "get a quote for X" come from decisions you have made or asked about. We do not invent them to create spending.
  • You can dismiss any suggestion, and OmnyID will not keep resurfacing it.

6. AI-generated content: useful, not infallible

OmnyID uses AI to draft suggestions, summaries and emails. Like any AI system, it can get a detail wrong, miss context, or phrase something in a way you would not. That is why every AI-drafted item is presented to you as a draft for review, not a final action (see section 7). We would rather you catch and correct something than trust it blindly, and the product is built around that assumption.

7. Nothing leaves your workspace without your say-so

OmnyID can prepare a draft email, a shortlist or a summary on your behalf, but it never sends, books or purchases anything automatically. Every sensitive action, such as contacting a vendor, sending an invitation or committing to a budget line, sits behind an explicit approval step you control. You can edit or cancel it before it goes anywhere.

8. Pricing

Our pricing is published, not negotiated per vendor deal or hidden behind a "contact us" wall. See the pricing page. Free and paid plans use the same recommendation approach described in section 2. Free-plan users are not shown different or less neutral recommendations to make up the difference.

9. Your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or planning data to any third party, including vendors, advertisers and sponsors. Advertising on OmnyID is not targeted using your wedding details. Full detail is in our privacy policy.

10. Your consumer rights (Australia)

Where Australian Consumer Law applies, nothing on this page limits the consumer guarantees you are entitled to, including guarantees that services are provided with due care and skill, and that any digital product is fit for the purpose it is described for. If you believe OmnyID has fallen short of those guarantees, contact us using the details below and we will work through it with you directly, before you need to escalate anywhere else.

11. Complaints and how we handle them

If a suggestion ever feels like it was steering you towards an unnecessary purchase or a vendor that was not the best fit, or you see something on this page that does not match what you experienced in the product, we want to know. Email us and we will investigate directly, including by reviewing the specific recommendation that concerned you, and respond within 5 business days.

12. Changes to this page

If we make a material change to how recommendations are generated, how we are funded, or any point on this page, we will notify you by email and within the product before the change takes effect, not just by editing this page quietly. The "last updated" date at the top always shows the current version.

13. Contact us

Questions about this page, or a specific recommendation you would like us to look at? Reach us at hello@omnyid.com and we will respond within 5 business days.
