1. What we collect

To build your wedding plan, we collect only what's needed to make it useful:

  • Account details — your name and email address, used to identify you and secure your account.
  • Planning details you provide — date or season, region, guest count range, budget range, priorities, and any cultural, religious, accessibility or dietary notes you choose to share.
  • Content you create in your workspace — budgets, guest lists, vendor shortlists, timelines, decisions, email drafts, and files you upload.
  • Usage information — when you use your workspace, basic technical data (device type, browser, approximate location from IP) used to keep the product working and secure. Visitors to this website are counted differently: see section 4.

We only ask for sensitive information — cultural, religious, family, or budget detail — when it directly unblocks a specific recommendation, such as menu planning or venue accessibility. We never infer these details from weak evidence like your name or suburb.

2. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Generate your planning responses — budgets, vendor shortlists, timelines and decision cards.
  • Create and maintain your response workspace and its files, so you can find and reuse them later.
  • Send you reminders for deadlines you've asked us to track (deposits, RSVP cut-offs, NOIM timing).
  • Prepare draft emails for your review — we never send anything without your explicit approval.
  • Keep your account secure and improve the reliability of the product.

We do not use your wedding details — including budget, guest list, or family information — to train third-party advertising systems, build advertising profiles, or target you with ads.

Wedding Understanding uses a hosted AI model. If you submit text, links, or photos to that tool, the submitted content leaves OmnyID and is sent to our configured model provider solely to create your requested result. Do not submit information you do not want processed this way.

Optional venue-quote extraction

Pasting or manually comparing a venue quote stays in your browser. If you deliberately choose Analyse quote, the text from that proposal is sent once through OmnyID to our configured hosted model provider to identify commercial terms and exact source wording. The model is asked not to retain submitted data. OmnyID does not store the proposal text or structured extraction on the public-tool server; it records only operational cost and abuse-control data without quote content.

Your browser may keep up to six structured extraction results under one-way text digests so unchanged text can be reused without another model call. The source proposal is not included in that cache. You can clear a proposal's saved analysis from the tool. Contextual extraction is optional: manual entry and deterministic comparison remain available if you decline or if the service is unavailable.

All displayed multiplication, minimum-spend handling, service-charge arithmetic and GST arithmetic is performed by browser code rather than the model. Allowing public Analytics does not allow proposal text, amounts or extracted fields to enter Analytics.

3. The free binder & planning tips emails

You can ask for the free Wedding Day Binder on this website without creating an account. We collect your email address to send the download links once, plus the request time, delivery status, whether you requested planning tips, and any broad campaign tags in the link that brought you here. We keep that request record for up to 12 months.

Planning tips are optional and double opt-in. We only add the address to the planning-tips list if you tick the separate box and then use the confirmation link in the binder email. The binder does not depend on consent to tips. Every tips email includes an unsubscribe option.

4. Optional website analytics

Public Wedding pages offer an optional Analytics choice. Analytics is off until you allow it, and declining does not limit the website, quote tools, account creation or your planner. You can reopen at any time. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track keep analytics off.

First-party measurement. When allowed, we count how this website is used so we can understand which channels bring couples here and which pages need clearer wording. This measurement uses no cookie, advertising identifier or fingerprint.

  • We record allowlisted page and section views, buttons used, a broad screen-size class, the first two letters of browser language, and engaged time rounded to five-second buckets.
  • We record allowlisted campaign tags, whether an advertising click ID was present but never its value, and a broad referrer class. Where relevant, we keep only the referring host, never its path or query.
  • We never put names, email addresses, form content, quiz answers or persistent identifiers in these events.

A random visit number exists only in session storage for the current tab. Identifiable first-party event rows are deleted after 30 days; aggregate day-level counts may be retained for trend reporting.

Ahrefs Web Analytics. When allowed, the page also loads Ahrefs. Ahrefs says its Web Analytics product uses no cookies, does not store raw IP addresses with analytics data, and does not track a visitor across days. It receives the page URL and parameters, referrer, browser and device information, screen size, language, and a city/country estimate derived from the request IP. It creates a daily pseudonymous visitor number and automatically counts page views, link clicks and form submissions. OmnyID uses the resulting aggregate reports to improve public pages, not to inspect wedding plans. Ahrefs is a separate provider and may process data outside Australia under its own privacy terms.

Changing Analytics from allowed to off reloads the page so no further optional events are sent. It cannot recall events already transmitted before withdrawal. The authenticated planner-data choice described below is separate: allowing public website analytics never permits administrators to read or analyse a wedding plan.

IP addresses and technical logs. Cloudflare processes network information to deliver and protect the site under its own privacy terms. Our application briefly records an IP address and timestamp to protect public forms and sign-in from abuse. These rate-limit records are deleted automatically within 48 hours.

5. The Wedding Animal Quiz

You can take the quiz without an account or email address. Answers stay in your browser unless you explicitly share a private comparison link or consent to bring the result into your OmnyID workspace. We count allowlisted starts, completions and shares but never send quiz answers or names through website measurement. The quiz is a playful planning profile, not a psychological or compatibility test.

6. We do not sell your data

OmnyID does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party, for any purpose, at any time. This applies to every plan, including the free tier. We don't monetise your information through advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners — our business is what couples pay us, plus commission and labelled advertising from vendors, never your data. See our disclosures.

If this ever changed, it would require a clear, opt-in change to this policy, not a quiet update to fine print.

7. Sharing & your control

Nothing in your workspace is shared automatically. Every share is explicit, scoped, and reversible:

  • Partner — share your whole plan, only when you invite them.
  • Family — share a specific artifact, like a run sheet or registry, never your full plan.
  • Planner — share what they need to coordinate, never your budget ceiling or private notes.
  • Vendors — a vendor only ever sees what's in an approved draft, such as a single email. They never see your guest list, budget, or other vendor comparisons.

You can review and revoke any active share from your workspace settings at any time.

Optional planner improvement statistics

During Wedding onboarding, an unticked choice asks whether authorised OmnyID administrators may use content-free progress statistics from that wedding. If enabled, the aggregate reporting can count durable actions such as completing the intake, saving a vendor, or resolving a decision. It excludes names, contact details, message and draft text, budgets, guest counts, quiz answers, files, private notes and the selected vendor itself.

This choice is stored against the signed-in user and wedding, not the browser. Declining does not change the planner. It can be changed in Documents; turning it off stops new private-plan progress events entering the administrator projection and removes that wedding's plan-level progress rows. Account, security, billing and delivery records that OmnyID must operate or retain are governed separately.

8. Security & encryption

Connections to OmnyID use encryption in transit, and production databases, object storage and credentials are protected by infrastructure access controls and encryption at rest. Some sensitive content is additionally stored in encrypted envelopes. This is not a promise that every field is end-to-end encrypted: the service must process some content and operational metadata to provide search, imports, email, automation and account functions, and server-managed integrations can use server-held encryption keys.

Where a workflow uses a recipient-held encryption key, the server does not receive that recipient's private key. Other records — including account and plan identifiers, timestamps, event types, delivery information and content-free product statistics allowed by the separate opt-in — remain available to authorised service processes. Production access is restricted to authorised operators who need it to run and protect the service.

9. Retention & deletion

Your workspace stays available after your wedding so you can revisit it whenever you like — we don't auto-delete your plan. If you close your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we're required to retain limited records (such as billing history) for legal or tax purposes.

If you use Wedding Understanding before signing in, its result (the generated theme and preference pairs, not your raw text or images) stays in your browser for up to 30 days. After you create an account or next sign in, OmnyID copies that result into your wedding plan and removes the browser copy after the save succeeds.

10. Your rights

Wherever you're located, you can:

  • Access and export your data in Markdown, HTML, Word-compatible, or CSV formats.
  • Correct inaccurate information directly in your workspace.
  • Delete specific artifacts, or your entire account, at any time.
  • Ask us what personal data we hold about you and how it's used.

11. Vendors & service providers

We use a small number of trusted infrastructure providers — for hosting, email delivery, payment processing, hosted AI processing, security, and basic service analytics — strictly to operate the service. Cloudflare may process request and security telemetry when you visit. These providers process data on our instructions and never receive more information than the task requires. We do not use data brokers or advertising networks.

12. Children's privacy

OmnyID Weddings is intended for adults planning a wedding. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we'll remove it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy — particularly anything affecting how your data is shared or used — we'll notify you by email and within the product before the change takes effect, not just by updating this page.

14. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@omnyid.com and we'll respond within 5 business days.
