Wedding Budget Reality Check

Start with one balanced allocation, then see which costs still need an answer in writing. The planning bands are guardrails for adjusting one category at a time; they are not percentages to add together.

How the budget method works

This calculator starts with one complete allocation whose category targets total exactly 100%. It is a planning model, not an Australian market-price promise. Entering a larger total changes the dollar amount in each category; it does not claim that a particular vendor will charge that amount. Your date, location, guest count, service level and package structure can all change real quotes.

The low and high percentages are adjustment bands. They show a practical area in which one category might move while you make trade-offs elsewhere. Because the bands overlap, their low points and high points are not separate budgets and must never be added together. If a venue package includes food, drinks, furniture or ceremony hire, record each cost once rather than counting it in two categories.

When you choose priorities, the calculator gives those categories modest extra room and rebalances the remaining targets without dropping a category below its floor. The useful output is therefore a starting allocation and a list of questions—not a forecast that every cost is known. Compare it with current written quotes, confirm tax, minimum spends, travel, overtime and cancellation terms, and revise the plan when evidence changes.
