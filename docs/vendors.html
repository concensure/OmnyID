Questions vendors ask

Before you join the pilot

Does OmnyID reply without us?

No. The intended workflow keeps outbound content reviewable by your team. It does not create a booking, price promise or commercial acceptance on your behalf.

Do we have to publish our full pricing methodology?

No. You can provide useful guidance—such as package bands, minimum spend and inclusions—while keeping margins, formulas and internal rules private.

What information can a couple share?

With permission, relevant context can include date, location, guest estimate, budget comfort, priorities and questions. Vendors should receive only what is needed for the enquiry.

Will it replace our CRM or sales team?

It is designed to reduce repetitive lead handling and quote confusion, not replace the personal knowledge that turns a good enquiry into a booking.

Can we correct package or capacity information?

That is the goal of a vendor-controlled response path: current facts should come from you, and uncertainty should stay visible until confirmed.
